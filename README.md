# Seconds: Island Clock — Support

Seconds displays the current second of your iPhone’s local system time, from **00 to 59**.

## Get help or report a bug

[Open a support request](https://github.com/William-Collins/seconds-island-clock-support/issues/new) or [browse existing reports](https://github.com/William-Collins/seconds-island-clock-support/issues).

A free GitHub account is required to post. This tracker is public. **Do not post email addresses, phone numbers, medical details, device identifiers, or other personal information.** Check screenshots before attaching them.

Please describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it. Optional details: iPhone model, iOS version, Seconds version, and whether the issue appears in the app, Dynamic Island, or Lock Screen. Share only what you are comfortable making public.

## Using Seconds

1. Open the app and tap **Start**.
2. Return Home to see seconds in the Dynamic Island on a compatible device.
3. Lock and wake the phone to see the Lock Screen card.
4. Open Seconds and tap **Stop** to end the session.

The name under the app’s Home Screen icon is **Seconds**.

## Common questions

**Why does a session end?** iOS limits Live Activities to at most eight hours and may dismiss them earlier. Reopen Seconds and tap Start for a new session. Sessions never restart automatically.

**Why do I see dashes or no seconds?** iOS controls visibility, reduced-luminance/Always-On states, and competing activities. Wake the screen or use the in-app clock to read current seconds.

**Why is Start unavailable?** A session may already be running, or Live Activities may be disabled for Seconds in Settings.

**Does Seconds play audio?** No. It does not play audio, activate an audio session, access the microphone, or access Bluetooth devices.

**Is this a timer?** The number is the current second of local system time, rolling from 59 to 00.
